Before we start

What is collected. Workouts (sets, reps, weights, distance, pace), heart-rate samples, sleep, SpO2, breathing rate, skin-temperature variation, steps and calories, from the apps and devices you connect. Nothing else from those accounts.

Who sees it. You. The server owner can technically access the database and promises not to look. Friends see only a weekly count, volume and distance, and only if you both opt in.

Where it lives. On this server, encrypted at rest for tokens, backed up nightly. It is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Leaving. Disconnect any app at any time. "Delete my account" removes every record immediately, backups included within 14 days.

Not medical advice. Readiness, load and calorie numbers are estimates for training decisions, not diagnoses.
